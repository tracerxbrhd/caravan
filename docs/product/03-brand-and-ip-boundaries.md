# 03. Brand and IP Boundaries

## Status

Accepted project boundary for design, implementation, documentation, marketing, and asset creation.

## Independent product rule

CARAVAN must be recognizable through its gameplay and its own identity, not through copied Fallout expression.

The project may independently specify and implement gameplay mechanics and rules concepts. It must not copy protected audiovisual, textual, software, or branding expression from Fallout: New Vegas or related Bethesda properties.

This repository is not an official, licensed, endorsed, or affiliated Fallout/Bethesda project.

## Do not use

Do not use or ship:

- `Fallout` or `Fallout: New Vegas` as product branding;
- Bethesda, Vault-Tec, Pip-Boy, NCR, Legion, franchise characters, faction marks, or logos;
- copied dialogue, tutorial wording, card descriptions, or rulebook prose;
- extracted textures, models, fonts, card art, music, sound effects, voice, icons, UI, or other game assets;
- copied source code, scripts, game files, or reverse-engineered proprietary implementation code;
- a near-identical reconstruction of the original UI or audiovisual presentation;
- marketing that could reasonably present CARAVAN as official or licensed.

## What should remain original

CARAVAN should develop its own:

- logo and typography;
- card faces/backs and table treatment;
- UI structure and component language;
- animations and effects;
- sound design and music, if any;
- tutorial and rules wording;
- terminology where unique source-specific wording would create unnecessary association;
- promotional art and store presentation.

## Visual direction

The accepted high-level visual direction is an original weathered frontier / trade-route card game rather than Fallout imitation.

Useful motifs include:

- wood;
- worn paper/card stock;
- metal details;
- dust and road wear;
- route diagrams;
- merchant stamps/seals;
- cargo/trade markings;
- practical tabletop objects.

These motifs should form a coherent CARAVAN identity, not a disguised reconstruction of Mojave/Fallout art direction.

## Product name

`CARAVAN` is the current working/product name because it is short, descriptive, and naturally connected to the game's trade-route concept.

Before commercial Android/iOS publication, perform appropriate store/trademark clearance and revisit differentiation if required. The repository name does not force the final store listing name.

## Legal review

Repository guidance is an engineering/product boundary, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before a significant commercial launch, obtain professional IP/trademark review if the risk profile justifies it.
